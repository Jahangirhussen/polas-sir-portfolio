About Me

Professional Overview

Academic background, research focus, and professional journey.

Mohammad Rashed Hasan Polas, PhD, is an Assistant Professor at the Strategic Research Institute, Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation, Malaysia. He holds a PhD in Management (Strategy, Entrepreneurship & Innovation) from Limkokwing Graduate School, Malaysia. His research spans strategy, entrepreneurship, sustainability, and emerging technologies (AI, IoT, blockchain, green energy), with 1,848+ Google Scholar citations across 88+ publications.

An academic career shaped by teaching, research, editorial leadership and interdisciplinary collaboration.
